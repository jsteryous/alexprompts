# The reading list

Every paper this engine has covered, and every paper it started and killed. The scout reads this
first so the publication never covers the same paper twice, and so a paper that failed the access
gate is not attempted again every week.

**This file is also the living artifact.** It is the running index of what the research actually
says about buying and selling a house, and it is the thing that will eventually be worth citing.
It grows by one entry per run, including the runs that publish nothing.

Entries are appended by the orchestrator in STEP 7, newest last.

---

## Format

```
### <Authors> (<year>), "<title>"
- STATUS: PUBLISHED <slug> | KILLED <gate that fired>
- ACCESS: the URL the full text was actually read from, or why it could not be
- THE TRICK: one line
- THE FINDING: one line, in units a person cares about
- HELD UP: what the later evidence says
- THE MODEL: the mechanism, where it generalizes, where it breaks
- GROUNDED IN: the real thing it was anchored to, and any computed figure with its input and date
- CALL ON THE RECORD: any claim made with a date to check it, or none
```

A KILLED entry is as valuable as a published one. It stops a future run from spending itself on a
paper that is paywalled everywhere, and it records which questions the literature cannot answer.
